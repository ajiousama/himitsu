# Japan IPTV CS Movie Research - Next Steps

This file tracks URL-free research steps for Japanese CS movie and cinema-adjacent channel groups.

## Scope

Included:

- Movie-focused CS channel groups
- Cinema-adjacent drama and stage groups
- Group-level system mapping
- URL-free source/system research

Excluded:

- Direct playback URLs
- Token, vhash, ac_tk, or session values
- p2p or p5p entries
- Detailed premium playback slug lists
- Credentialed or hidden sources

1. Confirm movie-specialty group coverage.
2. Cross-check whether entries appear in both `akariko/sankuria` and `haru blog` families.
3. Mark old-copy-only findings as legacy or exclude.
4. Keep detailed playback identifiers local-only, not in this repository.
5. After CS movie groups are stable, create separate URL-free registries for anime/kids, sports/hobby, and music/Asia.

## Decision rule

- `A_本命候補`: group appears across multiple recent-looking public GitHub sources or known system families.
- `B_保留候補`: group appears but needs more evidence or overlaps with another registry.
- `C_資料のみ`: EPG/channel-list-only evidence.
- `X_除外候補`: old-copy, p2p/p5p, token-heavy, or unknown-source material.
